TONIGHT · A LETTER TO ANGGUN
Dear Diary, I don't even know where to begin tonight. There are so many things going through my mind, and somehow, all of them lead back to one person. Anggun. It's been two days since we stopped talking to each other. Two days might not sound like a long time to some people, but for me, it feels like an eternity. I never thought that not talking to someone I love could make me feel this empty. I keep checking my thoughts, wondering what she's doing, whether she's okay, whether she's thinking about me too, or whether she's slowly getting used to not having me around. I miss her. God, I miss her so much. I miss the little things. I miss talking to her about random things that don't even matter. I miss hearing about her day. I miss the way our conversations used to make everything feel a little lighter. I miss having someone I could tell almost everything to. Now, there are so many things I want to say, but I don't know where to put them. So I just keep everything inside, hoping that somehow, the feelings will become easier to carry. But they haven't. I've been thinking about our problem over and over again. Replaying everything in my head, trying to understand where things went wrong. Maybe I said something I shouldn't have. Maybe I failed to understand her feelings. Maybe we both got caught up in our own emotions and forgot that we were supposed to be on the same side. I don't know. I just wish I could understand everything clearly. The hardest part is not knowing what she's feeling right now. I wonder if she's hurt. I wonder if she's angry. I wonder if she misses me the way I miss her. There are so many questions that I can't answer, and the silence between us somehow makes every question feel heavier. Since this morning, I've been feeling so overwhelmed. I've even had nosebleeds several times today, and honestly, I don't know what to make of everything. My mind feels exhausted. I keep trying to distract myself, but somehow, I always end up thinking about her again. It's like my thoughts have become a place I can't escape from. I wish I could tell her how I feel without making things worse. I wish I could explain that I never wanted us to end up like this. I wish I could tell her that even though we're having problems, my feelings for her haven't suddenly disappeared. I'm still here, still caring about her, still hoping that we can find our way back to each other. But I also know that love isn't just about missing someone. It's about understanding them, respecting their feelings, and admitting when we've made mistakes. I don't want to be someone who only thinks about his own sadness. I want to understand her pain too, even if it means hearing things that might hurt me. Sometimes I wonder if I'm doing enough. Am I being patient? Am I giving her the space she needs? Am I making things harder by constantly thinking about her? I don't want my fear of losing her to become a reason that I hurt her even more. I just wish things were different right now. I wish we could sit down and talk. Not argue, not blame each other, not try to prove who's right. Just talk. Two people who care about each other, trying to understand what happened. I wish we could be honest about our feelings and find a way to make things better. Anggun means a lot to me. Maybe I don't always express it properly, but she really does. And sometimes, I hate how difficult it is to put my feelings into words. There are moments when I want to say so much, yet all I can think of is how much I miss her. I don't know what tomorrow will bring. I don't know when we'll talk again. I don't know whether things will go back to the way they used to be. And maybe that's what scares me the most. The uncertainty. The feeling of not knowing whether someone you love is still holding on to the same things you are. But tonight, I just want to be honest with myself. I miss my girlfriend. I miss my Anggun. I miss us. And despite everything that's happening, I still hope that we can work things out. Not because I want everything to be perfect, but because I believe that what we have deserves understanding, patience, and effort from both of us. I hope she's okay tonight. I hope she's taking care of herself. I hope she's not hurting too much. And I hope that someday, when we look back at this moment, we'll realize that it was just a difficult chapter in our story, not the end of it. For now, I think I'll let my thoughts rest. I need to take care of myself, too. Byee Byeeee, diary. I miss her more than I can explain. Rei.
♡
written with a heart full of things to say · Rei
